How the sets were counted

  • A set is a lead a setter got onto a closer's calendar, counted once per setter per lead, on the day it was booked (ET). Duplicate lead records in Close with the same name are counted once.
  • Close finds a set two ways: the setter's Pre-Discovery Call form logged as "Discovery Call Scheduled", or a closer meeting created within 24 hours of the setter's call or intro call with that lead. A lead found either way counts. About 85% of sets show up both ways; the rest are mostly forms with no matching meeting (Quinn, Dave, Jordan) or meetings with no form (Bali, Jose, Yobani).
  • Inbound means the lead booked an intro call with the setter (the form's "Inbound / Scheduled Intro" type, or an intro meeting on the setter's calendar). Outbound means the setter reached the lead by dialing.
  • Dave is David Martin in Close; he logs the forms and meetings. Bali's Oct 5 sets for Matt Weiss and Dan Housley are left out, per Sheila's Oct 6 ruling.
  • Short term is the Sales pipeline's Next 7 Days and In Month Closing. Long Term No Timeline and Long Term Farm are shown together. Bad fit almost never shows up because closers rarely mark it; most weak calls are logged as long term instead.
  • Close data is as of Oct 28, 2026, 3pm ET. The tracker is the "Inputs" tab of Sales Tracker 2026, Setting team.
