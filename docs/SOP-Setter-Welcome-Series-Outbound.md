## 1. Purpose

Call every Welcome Series lead within 15 minutes of their Typeform submission.

**Who the leads are:** SMB Deal Hunter newsletter subscribers who filled out the Typeform and selected that they want us to reach out.

**Why Speed to Lead matters:** In the V1 test, leads reached within 15 minutes talked to the setter about a third of the time and produced half of all closer calls.

**How it works:**

1. Lead submits the Typeform
2. Close enrolls them in the [Welcome Series workflow](https://app.close.com/workflows/seq_6T4cQDe4oxhEh6LKVlCutV/), and a call task lands in your Close inbox. They also appear in the Welcome Series Smart View.

1. **You call**: under 15 minutes from submission (under 5 is better). The clock starts at submission, not when you notice the task.
2. Automated email at about 45 minutes.
3. Automated text about a day later.
4. No booking after the series → lead rolls into the Value Series.

## 2. Setup

- **Use a US caller ID.** If you don’t already use a US number, please make sure to switch it in your settings. In Close: *Settings > Communication > Phone & Voicemail > New Number*, search a US area code, then select it in *Phone Settings* (phone icon). Test-call your own cell to confirm. Send texts from the same number so leads can match them to the voicemail.
- **Record a voicemail drop.** *Settings > Communication > Phone & Voicemail > Voicemail Drop*. Record the Section 5 script (under 25 seconds). Close then logs "Left a voicemail" for you.
- **Power Dialer off.** Dial one lead at a time so you can double dial and leave voicemails.
- **Sort your queue newest first.** Open the Welcome Series Smart View, sort by *Created* (newest first). Every new sign-up gets called before you go back to older leads. Welcome Series tasks outrank everything else in your inbox.
- **Booking link ready.** Have your own calendar link handy for texts and set-to-self bookings.
- **Self tracker open.** The [Welcome Series Test sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1VJ4_Mqo1touv-DeEF5OazSP-26K97GwUSM7ngTqeNL4/edit?gid=1959171881#gid=1959171881) for your EOD numbers (Section 5).

## 3. On-Call SOP

Every live call runs four steps in order: **open, frame, qualify, objection handling, book.**

### Step 1: Open (first 10 seconds)

Don't introduce yourself. Say their name, mention the form they just filled out, and end on a question.

**Opener:** "Hey [Name], just saw that you signed up and you were asking for us to reach out about buying a business. Is that something you're actively pursuing?"

**Backup openers**

- "Hey [Name] — you just filled something out on our site about buying a business, so I wanted to follow up real quick. What's got you looking right now?"
- "[Name]? Hey — saw you just put your info in about buying a business. Figured I'd catch you while you're still at your desk. What prompted it?"

| If this happens | Say this |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone Call screener | "Hey, it's [your first name]." Don’t add company name because they doesn’t get you through the screener. |
| "Who is this?" | "[Name] with SMB Deal Hunter — we send the newsletter you signed up for. Just following up on the form." Then back to your question. |
| Doesn't remember the form | "No problem — you signed up for our newsletter on buying businesses and checked the box asking us to reach out. Are you actively looking, or more researching?" |

### Step 2: Frame

Keep it a quick, low-pressure follow-up. You're responding to their request and here to help, not chasing a sale. Tone: casual, unhurried, helpful.

### Step 3: Qualify

Follow the inbound process (5 questions, then a short 1-minute pitch). Listen for three things:

- **Goal:** what they want to buy, and why
- **Timeline:** looking now, or someday
- **Capital:** can they actually transact

### Step 4: Objection Handling

Follow the objection handling section in the inbound script, if you got any.

### Step 5: Book

| Read on the lead | Book to | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Serious**: clear goal, real timeline, capital, engaged | **a closer,** then do the handoff in Section 4A | **Pause** |
| **Interested,** not sure yet (default) | **your own calendar** for a longer qualifying call (on this set-to-self call, you can follow the inbound process) | **Pause** |
| **Not ready** | No booking. Set a next step (callback, resources), log it, text them your number | **Leave active** + say the script below |

**To pause:** open the lead → Workflows panel → Welcome Series → **Pause** (or **Remove**) → confirm it shows paused. Do it on the call or right after, not at end of day.

**Not-booked script:** "No worries, we don't have to do that right now, I'm just here to help. There's an automated email and text that will come out shortly. You can ignore them, but I want to make sure you have my contact information."

## 4. Off-Call SOP

### 4A: Handoff to the Closer (for the calls you set to a closer)

The handoff is what makes the lead show up. Do all four steps.

| When | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Right after booking | Send the **Setter to Closer Handoff** email (template in Close). |
| By end of the booking day | Leave your **setter notes in Close** (the lead's background) so the closer can read them before the call. Send every resource you promised. If you promised to pull deals for the closer to walk through, follow [these instructions](https://heymitch.notion.site/Setter-Handoff-PDF-how-setters-and-closers-use-it-3f8aee68e52f818ea71dd9fad252f198?source=copy_link). |
| Day before the call | Send a reminder about the call. |
| Morning of the call | If they haven't responded, send another personal reminder. |

### 4B: Log the call outcome

1. Log every touch in Close and pick one outcome for every call ([how-to Loom](https://www.loom.com/share/19a8d41d83954027abb828a1de9dcec0)):

| Outcome | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| Conversation: set to closer | You booked them with a closer |
| Conversation: set to self | You booked them on your own calendar |
| Conversation: not a fit | Real conversation, they don't qualify |
| Conversation: call back later | Real conversation, next step is a callback |
| No Answer: Left voicemail | No pickup, you left a voicemail |
| No Answer / Hung up | No pickup and no voicemail, or they hung up |
| Call screener: got through | Screener picked up and put you through |
| Call screener: didn't get through | Screener picked up and you didn't get through |

### 4C: No-Answer follow-ups: Three tries over two days

Every unreached lead gets all three tries. Leave the lead in the workflow throughout.

| Try | When | What to do | Call task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Immediately | Double dial (hang up, call again right away) → voicemail → text | Keep open |
| 2 | Same day, a few hours later, different time of day | Dial only. No second voicemail or text. | Remove after this try |
| 3 | Next day (new task appears) | Dial; reference yesterday's email. Voicemail OK. | Close out |

**Voicemail (try 1, under 25 seconds):** "Hey [Name], this is [Your Name] with SMB Deal Hunter. You just filled out our form about buying a business, so I was giving you a quick call while it was fresh. Nothing urgent, just wanted to see what you're looking for. I'll shoot you a text right after this so you've got my number. Talk soon."

**Text (right after the voicemail, same number):** *Hey [Name] — [Your Name] with SMB Deal Hunter. Saw you just filled out our form so I gave you a quick call. Let me know when's a better time to catch you! Here's my calendar if that's easier: [Your booking link]*

**Try 3 opener:** "Hey [Name] — [Your Name] with SMB Deal Hunter. You filled out our form yesterday and I sent you an email. Wanted to try you one more time."

Limits: one voicemail per try, one text per day. If you connect on any try, go back to Section 3

## 5. KPIs and Daily Scorecard

**Real-time notes in the team channel.** Short and rough is fine. Flag openers that land or die, exact objections in the prospect's words, reactions to the fast call, and anything broken (task didn't fire, lead not enrolled, workflow won't pause). Verbatim quotes beat summaries. If the workflow looks broken, flag it to Jay immediately.

**End of every dialing day** (voice notes are fine)

- **Numbers:** Fill out a self tracker in the [Welcome Series Test](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1VJ4_Mqo1touv-DeEF5OazSP-26K97GwUSM7ngTqeNL4/edit?gid=1959171881#gid=1959171881)
- **Working:** the language or timing getting the best response
- **Not working:** where calls die and why
- **Blockers:** anything that cost you the 15-minute window
- **One change** you'd make to this SOP

## 6. Short Reference

1. Task lands → dial within 15 minutes, newest leads first.
2. Screener → "Hey, it's [First Name]."
3. Picked up → assumed open, no introduction, end on a question.
4. Qualify on goal / timeline / capital:
   - Serious → book to closer → pause workflow → do all 4 handoff steps (4A)
   - Promising → book to your calendar → pause workflow
   - Not ready → next step + "let those fire" script → leave workflow active → text your number
5. No answer → double dial + voicemail + text → dial again later that day → dial next day.
6. Log a call outcome on every call (4B). Team channel notes as you go.
7. EOD update and self tracker daily.
