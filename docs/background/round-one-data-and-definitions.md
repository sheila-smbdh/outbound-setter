## How things were counted

- **Window:** Sep 17 to 28, 2026, the last 8 dialing days. Sep 16, Bali's first day, is left out except where noted.
- **Real Welcome Series call:** an outbound call by Bali to a lead created on or after Sep 14 (test launch) that had not already booked a call. Left out: 54 empty 0-second records Close logs next to real calls, and other calls such as calls tied to meetings he already had booked and older leads.
- **Real conversation:** verified from the call recordings. Every call of 10 seconds or more (513 calls) was transcribed, first 75 seconds, and labeled: live conversation, live pickup with no exchange, voicemail with a message, voicemail with no message, call-screening assistant, or unclear. The 32 calls longer than 4.5 minutes were counted as conversations without transcribing. Calls under 10 seconds count as no conversation. On Sep 29 the 132 calls lasting 22 to 49 seconds, whose first labels came without a transcript, were transcribed again; every label held. For the opener analysis, the first 45 seconds of all 114 pickups and all screened calls were transcribed. For the call-flow section, 16 conversations were also transcribed in full: the 9 that led to a booking and 7 long ones that didn't.
- **Label check:** 22 of the 23 calls Bali followed with his "great to meet you" booking text are labeled conversations, and every booking traced to him follows a call labeled a conversation.
- **What the recordings show for all 637 calls:** 103 live conversations, 11 live pickups with no exchange, 182 voicemails (a message was left on 6), 99 call-screening assistants, 224 calls under 10 seconds, 18 unclear.
- **Closer call credited to Bali:** a booking on the setter-to-closer (S2C) calendar, made after a conversation with Bali, with no other rep's call in between. Bookings come from Calendly and from Close's own calendar records, matched to leads by email, then phone. The Close records were added on Sep 29, after the Calendly pull turned out to miss 3 bookings.
- **Speed to first call:** time from the lead's creation in Close (the sign-up) to Bali's first call.
- **Tracker note:** this week's columns in the tracker are labeled one day off (Monday shows 9/27; Monday was 9/28).
