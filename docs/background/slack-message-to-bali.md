## Thread reply: Close steps

*Switch your caller ID to a US number*
1. In Close, go to *Settings > Communication > Phone & Voicemail* and click *New Number*. Search by US area code and pick one. (If you don't see the option, it needs an admin; ping me and we'll get it set up.)
2. Open your *Phone Settings* (the phone icon), click your current number (the 807 one), and choose the new US number.
3. Make a test call to your own cell and check that a US number shows up.
4. Send your texts from the same US number, so leads can match the text to the voicemail.

*Voicemail drop (saves time, and Close logs it for you)*
1. Go to *Settings > Communication > Phone & Voicemail > Voicemail Drop* and record the SOP voicemail from the playbook image (under 25 seconds).
2. On a call, wait for the beep, then click *Drop voicemail*. Close marks the call "Left a voicemail" automatically, so you don't need to type VM.
3. If the button isn't there, leave the voicemail yourself and start the call note with `VM`.

*Calling without the Power Dialer*
1. Open your Welcome Series Smart View and sort it by *Created*, newest first.
2. Click into the top lead and hit *Call*. If nobody answers, hang up and call once more right away (the double dial), then leave the voicemail and send the text.
3. Keep the call task open until you've made try 2 later that day. The try 3 task comes up the next day.
