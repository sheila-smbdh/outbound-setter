# Round two call script

For setters calling Welcome Series sign-ups. Built from Bali's round-one calls that booked closer calls, cut down to 5 to 8 minutes. Before round two starts, leadership should confirm the fit criteria in step 3 and the list in step 8.

## 1. Open (first 30 seconds)

"Hey [name], it's [setter] with SMB Deal Hunter. You asked us to reach out about buying a business. Is that something you're actively pursuing, or just exploring for now?"

If they're browsing: "Totally fair. Mind if I ask a few quick questions to see if a longer conversation is worth your time?"

If a call screener asks who's calling, give your first name only: "Hey, it's [setter]." In round one every call that got past a screener did it this way. Dial from a US local number.

## 2. Set the agenda

"I'm on the advisory team. Give me about five minutes to understand what you're after. If it looks like a fit, I'll set you up with one of our senior advisors."

## 3. Qualify

Ask all seven, in this order, and let them talk.

1. "How long have you been looking?"
2. "What's behind it: replacing your income or adding to it?"
3. "What kind of business, and where?"
4. "What would you want it to pay you each year?"
5. "How much could you put down? Is that cash, or tied up in a home or a 401(k)?"
6. "When would you want to start looking at deals?"
7. "Are you a US citizen? SBA loans now require it for every owner."

Book the closer when the answers meet the team's fit criteria for the S2C calendar (cash, timeline, SBA eligibility). If those criteria aren't written down yet, write them down before round two.

## 4. Book it live

"Based on what you've told me, this is worth an hour with [closer]. It's educational: there's no decision on that call, and you'll see the agreement so you can review it with your attorney. I'm looking at the calendar now. I have [day] at [time] or [day] at [time]. Which works better?"

Before hanging up:

- Confirm their email and book the S2C slot while they're on the line.
- "You'll get an invite from [closer]. I'm texting you a short video from Helen, our founder. Give it a thumbs-up so I know it came through."
- If leadership keeps it for round two, send Omar's Setter Resources email (subject "SMB Deal Hunter - Info Packet") within the hour.

## 5. If they ask about price

"It's a one-time fee of $20,000, with no commission at the end. [Closer] will walk you through exactly what's included. That's what the hour is for."

If they push back on paying upfront: "That's a fair question, and it's why the call with [closer] comes first. You'll see real deals we've sourced, the advisor team and the agreement before anyone asks you to decide. I'll send you examples of recent deals today."

If they want to see deals first, send the Deals Sourced link from the Setter Resources email and still book the call.

## 6. If they're not ready

"Totally understood. I'll send you our overview so you can see how it works. When should I check back?"

Ask the cash question before parking anyone, then create a Close task for the date they give.

## 7. No answer

- Voicemail, under 20 seconds: "Hi [name], it's [setter] from SMB Deal Hunter, following up on your request about buying a business. I'll send you a text now."
- Text: "Hey [name], this is [setter] with SMB Deal Hunter. You asked us to reach out about buying a business. Is this something you want to chat about, or were you just exploring?"
- Try 2 later the same day, try 3 the next day. Three tries in two days, every lead.

## 8. What to say, and what to leave out

Safe to say:

- One price: $20,000.
- Pro members close in about 8 months on average, compared with about 23 months for buyers searching on their own.
- Off-market deals go to Pro members and nobody else.

Leave out until leadership confirms them:

- Comparisons to Goldman Sachs, Harris Williams, Woodbridge or any bank
- Transaction totals, ad spend, or "a billion dollars" of advisor experience
- Guarantees, SBA rebates, or rolling the fee into the loan
- "Deals nobody's ever touched" or "exclusive to you"
- Anything suggesting the call or the program could be free

Never argue with a lead or text one in frustration. If a call goes badly, log it and move on.
